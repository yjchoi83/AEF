## 4. T3 TRANSFER — ROK → DPRK across the DMZ (LABELS=FALLBACK)
n_ROK=4540 / n_DPRK=3348 pixels; 6 classes (water, forest, grass-bare-shrub, wetland, cropland, built), WorldCover-balanced (ROK 810/810/810/490/810/810; DPRK 630/630/630/198/630/630); 5-fold GroupKFold on 0.12 deg (~13 km) blocks, 77 ROK / 94 DPRK blocks — never a random pixel split. Multinomial logistic probe.
- AEF: ROK in-region OA **0.842±0.020** (folds .807/.833/.847/.860/.863) → DPRK agreement **0.750** vs WorldCover, 0.631 vs Dynamic World; apparent gap **+0.093**.
- S2 baseline: ROK OA **0.715±0.018** (folds .692-.746) → DPRK 0.677 vs WorldCover, 0.582 vs Dynamic World; gap +0.038.
- Concordance ceiling: WorldCover-vs-DynamicWorld agreement *on the DPRK side* is only **0.674**. The ROK-trained AEF probe (0.750) therefore agrees with WorldCover BETTER than the two independent products agree with each other, whereas S2 (0.677) merely matches that ceiling. The +0.093 apparent gap is far smaller than the 0.326 inter-product disagreement, so the residual is dominated by label noise, not by domain shift.
- Distribution shift: TV(predicted, WorldCover) = 0.154 (AEF) vs 0.158 (S2) — mild and comparable.
- Label-free shift on the AEF sphere: mean max-cosine to ROK class centroids 0.874 (ROK) → 0.837 (DPRK), drop only 0.037. vMF concentration kappa ROK/DPRK: water 416/235, forest 170/183, grass-bare 142/178, wetland 298/315, cropland 237/285, built 189/219 — DPRK classes are equally or MORE concentrated except water (turbid rivers/reservoirs), i.e. the border is a management discontinuity, not a representation-space blow-up. This supports TC06's hypothesis that ROK→DPRK loss is label-semantics-driven rather than covariate-shift-driven.
- EXPLICIT LIMIT: none of the above is accuracy. Stratified VHR interpretation of ~300 design-based points with two interpreters is the real check and is deliberately out of scope for a smoke test.

**Reviewer 2 (most damaging):** "Your DPRK 'accuracy' is agreement with Dynamic World and WorldCover, neither of which is validated in DPRK; you have measured product concordance, not accuracy, so no forest-campaign assessment follows." Preempted three ways: (i) we publish the product-vs-product ceiling (0.674) next to the probe's 0.750 and treat the difference as a bound on label noise vs domain shift, never as accuracy; (ii) the primary accuracy and area claims rest on a design-based stratified VHR reference sample with CIs and inter-interpreter agreement, with the products used only for stratification and as shift diagnostics; (iii) the dated state projects act as independent natural validation events with pre-registered expected years, and we report the failures (Hwasong) alongside the hits.
**ETHICS / DATA PROVENANCE:** civilian land use only — the 6 harmonised classes contain no facility, installation or infrastructure category, and no site is characterised beyond its publicly announced civilian function; Jungpyong and Ryonpho enter only as agricultural conversions of former airfields already reported by CSIS Beyond Parallel and 38 North. All inputs are public (AEF, WorldCover v200, Dynamic World, Sentinel-2 L2A, Copernicus DEM) and every event date carries a public gray-literature source with a [V]/[U] tag. Analysis stays at strategic 10 m annual scale; no military-site identification, no tracking, no real-time use. **DUAL-USE FLAGGED:** construction-timing detection could in principle serve non-humanitarian monitoring; mitigated by publishing county-level aggregates and detection-lag statistics rather than site coordinate lists, and by framing outputs for KFS inter-Korean forestry cooperation, the Ministry of Unification and FAO/WFP food-security assessment.
