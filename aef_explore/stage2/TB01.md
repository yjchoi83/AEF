Block-wise rho (>=25 px, >=5 months): BR n=17 mean -0.34 sd 0.27 [-0.91,+0.06]; CG n=27 mean -0.47 sd 0.25. Detection lag: thresholding
ang(e_{Y-1},e_Y) at the stable-forest p90 (11.4/8.9 deg) flags **99.2% (BR) / 98.7% (CG)** of 2021 clearings already in year Y; even December events
give 27.0 / 16.4 deg of change. READ: month is recovered monotonically, but the strict (12-m)/12 geodesic law is FALSIFIED — w>1 for Jan-May (year-Y
sits *past* e_{Y+1}: fresh bare/burn scar vs Y+1 regrowth) and 0.31-0.41 of the year-Y vector lies off the (e_{Y-1},e_{Y+1}) great circle. Replacement
model: 3-state arc (forest -> bare/burn -> early regrowth). AEF beats S2 on calibration (2x lower MAE) and spread (5-7x tighter per-month sd).
## 4. Verdict — N 3 | R 3 | F 3 | A 3 | V 3 | J 2 = **17/18**. PASS (falsified law + replacement model + rule).
A=3: month-graded mixing is a property of the annual product; S2 recovers month far more noisily. J=2: one sharp finding = GRSL-safe; TGRS/ISPRS needs
multi-region + TESSERA baseline + a calibrated lag/attribution rule. Risks: (1) RADD Date is detection, not event, date -> month labels biased late
(cross-date GLAD-S2/DETER, latency-deconvolution sensitivity). (2) Month confounded with dry season, burn date, S2/S1 observation density - the S2
baseline's rho -0.57 proves the confound is real. (3) Block spread sd 0.25-0.27 with sign flips -> a population-level, not per-pixel, month estimator.
Reviewer 2: "w-vs-month is dry-season / observation-calendar seasonality, not within-year mixing." Preempt: replicate across contrasting calendars
(Para unimodal vs DRC bimodal) and >=3 years; add per-pixel S2/S1 valid-observation counts and MCD64A1 burn date as partial-correlation covariates;
and note the S2 composite, sharing that same observation calendar, is 2x worse in MAE.
