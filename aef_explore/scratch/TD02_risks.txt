## 4. Risks and Reviewer 2
R1 LABEL CIRCULARITY (highest): MapBiomas age is itself a Landsat-trajectory product, so "one embedding year beats trajectory dating" is unclaimable against its own label source; and AEF's training inputs include Landsat, so part of any skill may be re-reading trajectory-shaped information. Mitigation: the claim is reframed as INFORMATION CONTENT of a single annual embedding (an upper bound on what one epoch retains), the trajectory product is called the reference not the competitor, and the SATURATION age is confirmed against non-Landsat, non-GEDI structure (NSIDC ATL08 h_canopy + Sustainable Landscapes Brazil ALS).
R2 RIGHT-CENSORING at the 1986 record start: ages >=36 in 2022 are lower bounds, and the 26-35 bin is only 2.6% of ROI area, exactly where saturation must be located. Mitigation: censored pixels excluded; the saturation age is reported with its bin n; a second ROI in the Atlantic Forest (longer clearing history, more old regrowth) is added in Stage 3 to thicken the old tail, and the saturation statement is made as an interval, not a point.
R3 SPATIAL AUTOCORRELATION + AGE-vs-SITE-QUALITY CONFOUNDING: age classes are spatially clustered (single clearing events), and older regrowth is systematically on worse soils / steeper / more remote land (the parcels not re-cleared), so a regressor can score on site quality rather than successional stage. Mitigation: >=10 km GroupKFold blocks (never random pixel split); Stage 3 adds a stratification on soil/terrain/distance-to-road and reports R2 within terrain strata, plus a permutation of block labels as a null.
REVIEWER 2 (most damaging): "Your target is a Landsat-trajectory product and your predictor was trained on Landsat, so a high R2 shows only that the embedding memorised the trajectory features that generated the label, and a low R2 shows only label noise -- either way the paper measures the label, not the forest." PREEMPTION: (i) the mandatory single-year Sentinel-2 baseline on identical samples isolates how much is generic single-epoch spectral/structural signal vs embedding-specific; (ii) the saturation age is validated against ALS/ICESat-2 canopy structure, which never entered the MapBiomas trajectory, so the ceiling is a property of the forest, not the label; (iii) the 2-year embedding-pair contrast quantifies how much *additional* trajectory information a second epoch injects, which directly bounds the memorisation channel; (iv) the paper's headline claim is deliberately an information-content upper bound, falsifiable by the ALS check, not an accuracy-beats-trajectory claim.
