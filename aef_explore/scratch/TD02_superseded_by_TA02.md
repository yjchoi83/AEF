## 1. Literature (5 [V]; DOIs resolved in-session via Crossref, arXiv id verified via arXiv API)
- arXiv:2605.05547 (2026) *Characterizing Brazilian Atlantic Forest Restoration Outcomes with AlphaEarth Embeddings* [V] — NEAREST AEF work; 1,729 Sao Paulo restoration sites. It builds a **"Reference Trajectory Embedding"**: cosine similarity to mature-secondary-forest reference sites as a *relative* success score. It does **NOT** regress stand age, reports **no age saturation analysis**, and concludes embeddings "may require further fine-tuning to capture and predict site metadata beyond LULC" — i.e. the age-decodability question is open and its own abstract flags it as unresolved.
- 10.1038/s41597-020-00600-4 (2020, Scientific Data) *Benchmark maps of 33 years of secondary forest age for Brazil* [V] — THE trajectory-dating baseline (annual Landsat/MapBiomas LULC trajectory, 30 m, 1986-2018). MapBiomas Collection-9 secondary-vegetation-age is the same lineage.
- 10.1038/s41467-021-22050-1 (2021, Nature Comm.) *Large carbon sink potential of secondary forests in the Brazilian Amazon* [V] — age-dependent regrowth curves; consumes exactly the age layer this topic tries to produce from one embedding year.
- 10.1038/s41586-022-05679-w (2023, Nature) *The carbon sink of secondary and degraded humid tropical forests* [V] — pantropical sink estimate whose largest uncertainty term is stand age / regrowth stage.
- 10.1038/s41558-025-02355-5 (2025, Nature Climate Change) *Protect young secondary forests for optimum carbon removal* [V] — the policy hook: removal rate peaks in young stands, so an age map is the decision-relevant layer, and most tropical countries have none.
GAP (2 sentences): Secondary-forest age is currently obtainable only by walking a 30+ year annual Landsat trajectory, which is impossible where the archive is cloud-broken or short, and no one has measured how much of that age information is already compressed into ONE year of a general-purpose 10 m embedding, nor where that information ceiling lies in age. The nearest AEF paper measures similarity-to-reference, not age, and explicitly leaves age/metadata prediction unresolved.
STRONGEST COMPETITOR: 10.1038/s41597-020-00600-4 (trajectory dating). DIFFERENCE: it *is* the trajectory method (and the label source), so this topic does not compete with it on accuracy — it asks the orthogonal representational question of single-epoch information content and its age ceiling, and validates the ceiling against structure data the label source never saw.

## 2. Data / asset verification (GEE checks 1-3, project alpha-earth-app)
- `projects/mapbiomas-public/.../mapbiomas_collection90_secondary_vegetation_age_v1` = single Image, 38 bands `secondary_vegetation_age_1986..2023`; band `..._2022` sampled OK in ROI, values 1-37 years. Calendar-year semantics, matching AEF year-Y = calendar year Y -> `..._2022` pairs with AEF 2022 with NO re-binning (contrast: PRODES Aug-Jul would need it). Age is the stand age AT the end of year 2022, so the AEF 2022 layer summarizes observations of that same stand-age state.
- Age distribution in ROI (90 m histogram, %): 1-3 7.5 | 4-6 16.9 | 7-10 21.8 | 11-15 31.6 | 16-25 19.7 | 26+ 2.6. Naturally right-skewed toward young stands, so stratified sampling is mandatory (unstratified would be ~46% under 10 y).
- RIGHT-CENSORING: 2022-1986 = 36, so ages 36-37 are "regrowth already present in the first year of record" and are censored, not measured. Handling: ages >=36 EXCLUDED from training and evaluation; top bin capped at 26-35; the saturation claim is therefore made strictly inside the uncensored support and any conclusion at 26-35 is flagged as thin (2.6% of area).
- INDEPENDENT STRUCTURE REFERENCE (checked in-session): ICESat-2 ATL08 is NOT in the GEE catalog (`NASA/ICESat-2/ATL08`, `projects/sat-io/open-datasets/ICESAT2/ATL08*` all NOT FOUND). So the independent validation must be ingested off-GEE: NSIDC ATL08 `h_canopy` granules (icepyx/NSIDC subsetter) and/or Sustainable Landscapes Brazil airborne-LiDAR transects (ORNL DAAC, Amazon + Atlantic Forest, ~1500 flight lines). GEDI, ESA/ETH canopy height and Meta canopy height are ALL excluded as validation: AEF ingests GEDI-derived canopy-height rasters and the two products are GEDI-trained, so none is independent.
