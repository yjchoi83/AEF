## Notes and confounders to carry into Stage 2
- Circularity guard: any AEF-vs-GEDI structure result must be reported alongside a non-GEDI reference (ICESat-2 ATL08 or national ALS); otherwise the finding is unfalsifiable. This is the single biggest Reviewer-2 risk across TD01/TD02/TD06.
- Label-year alignment: AEF year Y = calendar year (verified in 00_landscape.md); DETER/PRODES need Aug-Jul re-binning, MapBiomas and TMF are calendar-year.
- TD02 label noise: MapBiomas age is itself trajectory-derived from Landsat, so "single-year beats trajectory" cannot be claimed against its own label source — frame as information-content, and use independent structure for the saturation claim.
- TD03 feasibility cap: GEE carries Sentinel-1 GRD only, so interferometric coherence is out of scope; residual features must be backscatter/temporal-texture based.
- All pilots: spatial block CV (>=10 km blocks / separate UTM tiles), mandatory baseline = annual cloud-masked S2 median composite + same regressor.
- Journal targets: TD01 -> RSE; TD02 -> RSE or Global Change Biology; TD03 -> ISPRS J. / JSTARS; TD06 -> IEEE TGRS (or GRSL if the discontinuity is the whole result). MDPI excluded throughout.
