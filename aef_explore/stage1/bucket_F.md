- Gaps attacked: TF02 opens a new sub-gap under **G8/G5** (protocol-level over-optimism specific to embedding features, with a measured minimum block size); TF03 is the direct **G8** attack (systematic multi-target AEF failure modes); TF04 is the direct **G5** attack (design-based area estimation and variance on embedding products); TF05 is a **G5/G8** side-gap (reference-data quality ceiling on reported gains). No PASS candidate depends on G1-G4, G6, G7.
- Threat map: TF02 is most threatened by "How to Embed Matters" [U] and "Beyond Accuracy" (arXiv:2608.16614 [V]) if either already reports random-vs-spatial split deltas — check both full texts first in Stage 2; TF03 by "Harvesting AlphaEarth" (arXiv:2601.00857 [U]) and Better Together (arXiv:2605.18667 [V]), which cover multi-task comparison but not failure prediction; TF04 by the calibration paper (2608.16614 [V]), accuracy-side only, and by any Olofsson-lineage stratification paper; TF05 by "On the foundations of Earth foundation models" (10.1038/s43247-025-03127-x [V]), which argues the point qualitatively but measures nothing.
