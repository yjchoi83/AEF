## Gaps attacked by PASS candidates
- G6 (degradation intensity / secondary-forest age with AEF) is hit hardest: TA01 (intensity gradient + GEDI structure loss), TA02 (age regression and saturation), TA03 (dry-woodland degradation, a G6 extension outside moist forest).
- G3 (static/climate-input dependence vs transfer) is probed as a secondary axis by TA01 (Amazon->Congo head transfer), TA03 (ERA5-Land rainfall confounding) and TA04 (Amazon->Ghana ASGM transfer, the cleanest cross-continent test); G8 (AEF vs simple spectral baselines) is the mandatory-baseline layer in TA01-TA03.
- G5 (design-based area estimation / UQ on embedding products) is carried by TA05 as the primary contribution; not carded here: wetland/peatland (Cuvette Centrale) and frontier-road candidates were dropped pre-card - peat drainage has no annual independent reference at 10 m, and narrow roads are sub-pixel for a pixel-level 10 m embedding, so both would have scored V<=1.
