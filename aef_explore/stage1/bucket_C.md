## Notes (gaps attacked and threats)
1. PASS candidates attack G3 head-on (TC01 mechanism test, TC03 predictive shift metric), G8 partially
   (TC02 and TC03 quantify where AEF stops beating spectral composites), and G4 (TC06, first AEF work on DPRK);
   TC04 opens a bucket-C sub-gap not in G1-G8: retrieval-based active learning for rare targets.
2. Most threatening comparison papers: "Geospatial foundation models enable data-efficient tree species mapping"
   (SRS 2026 [V]) pre-empts generic label-efficiency claims, so TC02 must own the Global South + n* mechanism;
   SpectralX (ISPRS 2026 [V]) already offers a domain-generalization fix, so TC01/TC03 must stay diagnostic
   (mechanism + label-free predictor), not method-competitive; Better Together (arXiv:2605.18667 [V]),
   How to Embed Matters [V] and AEF-vs-TESSERA LCZ (arXiv:2606.20034 [V]) already cover model-vs-model accuracy.
3. Reviewer-2 risks: TC01 confounds static-input removal with generic dimensionality loss (preempt with a
   random-subspace ablation of equal rank); TC03 could be dismissed as rediscovering MMD (preempt by showing
   the vMF-specific statistic beats generic MMD at rank correlation with measured transfer loss); TC06 has no
   independent DPRK truth (preempt with a pre-registered stratified VHR sample and dual-annotator agreement).
