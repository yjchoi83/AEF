## Notes

- **arXiv:2608.16614** (2026-08-17) [V] benchmarks *calibration* of 16 frozen encoders on 4 classification + 5 segmentation datasets under two corruption/shift axes; UQ methods tried are temperature scaling, deep ensembles and a Gaussian-process probe, plus confidence-based selective prediction. It contains **no conformal prediction, no distribution-free coverage guarantee, no spatial-block shift, and no design-based area estimation or policy measurand** — so TG01/TG04/TG05 are not pre-empted; it is instead the strongest verified *motivation* (EO-pretrained encoders drift into overconfidence under shift, abstention does not rescue them).
- **arXiv:2607.08945** (2026-07-09) [V] is a landscape-stratified accuracy assessment in **Cote d'Ivoire only**: 2,821 independently interpreted reference points across tree-cover-density and fragmentation gradients, comparing 0.5 m Pleiades (F1 0.92), TESSERA (0.86), AEF (0.82), Sentinel-2 (0.76) and four public cocoa products (Kalischek best, 0.83). It reports **F1 by stratum, not design-based area estimates with CIs**, does not cover Ghana, coffee/rubber, GEM-Forest 2020, or the 2020 EUDR-cutoff decision consequence — which is exactly where TG03/TG04 sit. It does kill any plain "map cocoa with AEF" candidate (TG06).
- **No independent accuracy assessment of the FDaP 2025b commodity maps was found** (72 OpenAlex hits on EUDR/cocoa validation; closest are 10.3390/rs17173012 on global-forest-map suitability for EUDR [V, MDPI, excluded venue but citable] and 10.1038/s41597-025-06097-z "Natural forests of the world — a 2020 baseline" [V], a rival EUDR baseline usable as cross-reference). Gaps attacked: **G5** (design-based area estimation + UQ on embedding products) by TG01, TG02, TG03, TG04; **G2** (stable-land drift) by TG05 in its MRV-consequence form; **G8** (AEF failure modes vs simple baselines) partially by TG03's stratum-wise omission analysis.
