## 2026-09-06 — TB01-P1: expert-dated event set and registration-probability curve (annual embeddings)
- **Package**: DETER 2021 × MapBiomas Alerta bracketed events in Pará BR-163 (200×200 km) and Roraima; annual-AEF registration curve vs post-event Sentinel-2 clear count; RADD latency; alert observability bias.
- **Expected**: ≥300 events/region; P(registered) rising with post-event clear observations (H1); no region effect once count is controlled (H2); ≥95 % registration at ≥5 clear obs (H3). Pass = H1 ∧ H3.
- **Observed — event set**: A 252, B 31 (both short of 300). The ≤62-day bracket is the binding filter (759→252, 276→31); 139 events fall inside the 80×80 km custom-embedding boxes.
- **Observed — thresholds**: stable-forest p90 angular change τ_A = 11.39° (n = 6,256 px), τ_B = 19.13° (n = 5,655 px); event medians 37.7°/39.5°.
- **Observed — curve**: registration is saturated — A 251/252, B 31/31. P(registered) is 0.994–0.998 flat across post-event counts 0–10 in both regions; observed counts never fall below 3 (A) or take the values 1–2 anywhere.
- **Observed — latency/bias**: RADD covers only 48 %/55 % of events; median latency 35.5 d (A) vs 70.0 d (B), share ≥2 months 0.29 vs 0.59. Bias scan over 200 random boxes: Pearson −0.088 [−0.158, −0.006], Spearman −0.008 [−0.150, 0.141].
- **Verdict**: **H1 FAIL, H2 rejected in sign but ≤0.4 pp in size, H3 PASS → overall FAIL.** H1 fails on a ceiling, not a contradiction: the annual embedding registers essentially every bracketed clearing, and the bracket criterion itself strips out the low-observability events the hypothesis needs.
- **Caveat**: the explanatory variable is truncated by construction; counts 0–3 in the probability table are extrapolation, and only 10 (A) / 11 (B) 0.5° blocks back every CI.
- **Next**: to test H1 at all, the event set must admit long-bracket (cloudy) events — either relax the 62-day rule with bracket length as a covariate, or date events from RADD/sub-annual custom embeddings inside the two 80×80 km boxes rather than from optical brackets.

## 2026-09-06 — TB01-P1c: SAR-dated event population (progress)
- **Framing**: P1 failed **by design, not by refutation** — its optical-bracket selection (Alerta before/after ≤ 62 d) could only admit already-well-observed events, truncating the explanatory variable and pinning P(registered) on a ~0.996 ceiling. P1c **pre-registers a SAR-dated design** (PLAN.md, committed before any computation) to recover the low-observation regime.
- **Step 1 done**: S1 GRD IW VV+VH, both passes, 2020-07-01…2022-03-31, polygon-interior mean at 30 m for all 1,916 DETER 2021 CR+VEG events ≥ 1 ha (A 1,247 + B 669; 845 in the custom boxes). 219,318 observations, median 97 (A) / 137 (B) per event. Joint z-scored single-breakpoint fit after per-relative-orbit de-meaning: median R² 0.21 (A) / 0.20 (B), median VV/VH shift −0.39/−0.37 dB (A) and −0.63/−0.72 dB (B); 77 % (A) / 88 % (B) of SAR dates land in 2021.
- **Step 2 done — PRE-REGISTERED GATE FAILED, STOPPED.** On the 283 P1 bracketed events: median |SAR date − bracket midpoint| = **27.0 d** [23.0, 35.0] (required ≤ 20) and **27.9 %** [0.234, 0.346] fall inside [before, min(after, view_date)] (required ≥ 70 %). Chance agreement ≈ 9 %, so the fit beats chance ~3× and still misses both bars. Four unregistered diagnostic variants (VH-only, VV-only, no de-meaning, restricted window) reach at best 24 d / 31.4 % — the failure is a property of single-breakpoint dating on polygon-mean S1 backscatter, not one implementation choice. **Steps 3–5 not run**, per PLAN.md.
- **Steps 3–5: NOT RUN** (gate). No post-SAR-date clear counts, no H1′/H3 model, no interval-censored check — H1′ and H3 remain untested in P1c.
- **Step 6 done — overturns P1 §5.** RADD first alert taken as the min date **anywhere inside the polygon** rather than at the centroid: coverage rises from 48 %/55 % to **97.6 % (A, 1,217/1,247)** and **85.4 % (B, 571/669)**, and median latency vs the P1 bracket midpoint flips from +35.5/+70.0 d to **−11 d** [−18, −8] (A) and **−7 d** [−19, −6] (B), with **0 %** of events ≥ 2 months late in either region. P1's RADD-latency row was a centroid-sampling artefact and should not be cited.
- **SAR-independent descriptive**: registration over the full 1,916-event population (same τ as P1) is A **0.9928** [0.9823, 0.9981], B **0.9492** [0.9193, 0.9764] — Roraima sits ~5 pp below P1's 31/31 ceiling, so a low-observation regime does exist once optical-bracket selection is removed. Exploratory (not pre-registered): registration by 2021 total clear-obs quartile is monotone, 0.950 → 0.980 → 0.989 → 0.995.
- **Verdict**: SAR dating **NOT ADOPTED**. P1c delivers the un-selected event population, a corrected RADD result, and evidence that the H1 signal is real — but no per-event date accurate enough to test H1′.
- **Next**: date events by a method the gate can pass — e.g. RADD first-alert date inside the polygon (now known to cover ~90 % of events and to precede the optical midpoint), or a multi-breakpoint / per-track S1 fit validated against the same tightened interval before use.
